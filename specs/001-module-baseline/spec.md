### Sarsaparilla, Menacing, and Your Turn

- **FR-004 — Sarsaparilla Surprise.** A selected actor with available strikes supplies the chosen strike modifier; the user enters the natural d20 from the missed strike and optionally targets an actor for AC comparison. Draw, Hit and Stand resolve the existing card game: natural total 21 is a critical hit, exceeding it is a critical miss, otherwise the modified total is compared with available target AC or left to GM judgment. Closing Hit/Stand stands; cancelling setup does not start play. Queued socket animations reach connected clients and a chat card records the result with expandable card details. The feature does not automatically reroll a native Strike or apply damage. Sources: [Sarsaparilla factory](../../src/scripts/features/sarsaparilla-surprise.ts), [hotbar wrapper](../../src/packs/astillon-player-macros.db/Sarsaparilla%20Surprise.json).

- **FR-005 — Menacing.** A GM/token owner toggles a token-local cosmetic status through the normal status palette or API. Its saved flag synchronizes the looping animation, which follows token movement/size and is recreated when the scene returns. It adds no PF2e condition, modifier, actor effect, or replacement overlay icon; linked tokens may differ. Player visibility excludes hidden, unseen, off-level and detection-only tokens. Shared animation assets are reused and token sprites are removed on disable/deletion/teardown. Source: [Menacing factory](../../src/scripts/features/menacing.ts).

- **FR-006 — Your Turn playback.** GM-controlled world settings enable automatic cut-ins, off by default, and configure shared presentation. Changes of combatant/round/turn in the viewed combat announce every combatant to viewers, irrespective of actor ownership. Art falls back through custom actor/base-actor art, actor/token images and a generic portrait. Portrait width is an aspect-preserving target; no silent fit-down cap negates enlargement. Automatic cards start their full timed presentation after art preparation/decoding, discard obsolete requests, and optionally play one local Interface-channel stinger. A playing stinger can outlast the card; replacement, explicit cancellation, disable and teardown stop it, and expired pending audio does not begin later. Missing audio does not block the card. The registered legacy Hold setting is not evidence of a variable animation duration: current timed playback uses the source's 2.6-second timeline. Source: [Your Turn factory](../../src/scripts/features/your-turn.ts).

- **FR-007 — Your Turn editor.** Owners/GMs can edit art, color, sound and portrait placement through the sheet control and native file picker. Editor Preview is local, held until stopped/replaced/closed, and available while automatic playback is disabled. Placement/color edits update the existing preview without replaying sound or saving flags; separate editors retain separate drafts. Save writes these fields together; Cancel discards drafts. Base-actor inheritance, explicit Reset and clearing media retain their existing semantics. Migration seeds only absent world settings from the eligible primary GM's saved preferences, preserving explicit world choices. Source: [Your Turn factory](../../src/scripts/features/your-turn.ts), [operating instructions](../../README.md#your-turn).

### Blaster encounter tools

- **FR-008 — Straight blasts.** The active GM selects one owned Blaster token in a PF2e Foundry V14 square-grid scene, chooses a charge and existing spectrum, then aims with one click. The immediate blast is a fixed-length straight native line Region at the selected width, not a waypoint path. Cancellation creates no area. Placement revalidates source identity, ownership, scene and unchanged position after waits, and rolls back created Regions if required state persistence fails. The card exposes native PF2e save/damage links; the GM resolves saves, HP, riders, cover and line of effect, affecting each creature once and excluding Blaster. Clear Blast Areas removes that token's immediate/recoil Regions, separately from Finale. Action/recharge/frequency text is guidance, with no hard combat/turn/frequency invocation gate or automated action debit. Sources: [Blaster factory](../../src/scripts/features/blaster.ts), [placement](../../src/scripts/features/blaster-placement.ts), [current mechanics](../../src/scripts/features/blaster-model.ts).

- **FR-009 — Grand Finale.** Mark selects an existing spectrum and an eight-point connected warning path with the shipped segment limits/width. It deals no warning damage and remains fixed after the caster moves. A completed mark replaces that token's old plan; cancelled placement preserves it. The saved in-combat plan fires at the following global round, while out-of-combat plans remain manual-only. Fire overrides timing; Clear removes only that token's Finale plan/Regions. Firing presents one save/damage card for the path, with per-creature application once across overlaps. Persisted state, serialized hooks and recovery guards suppress duplicate publication; uncertainty is not permission to replay damage output. Source: [Finale helper](../../src/scripts/features/blaster-finale.ts).

- **FR-010 — Recoil and sheet abilities.** Recoil Stride explicitly arms one subsequent normal move, then places the rearward line from its departure point using the chosen spectrum. Unarmed movement does not fire; cancellation and stale/mismatched movement invalidate the pending action. Movement distance, costs and legality stay GM-adjudicated. Native Quickened, Fragile Ego and Feed the Ego remain the user's shipped sheet mechanics; their manual damage, effect placement, thrall consumption and healing are not newly automated by adoption. Sources: [recoil helper](../../src/scripts/features/blaster-recoil.ts), [Blaster actor](../../src/packs/astillon-blaster-actors.db/Blaster.json).

- **FR-011 — Blastback.** Start arms a charge or opens its editable bank. Native PF2e damage taken after mitigation, including temporary-HP interactions, feeds the bank; arbitrary HP edits are not inferred as damage. Warning/charging visuals follow the source token; changing the cosmetic charge visual does not change the bank. Release occurs at the next own turn or the existing world-time fallback, and Fire/Clear provide GM overrides. Release publishes flat banked force damage with the native basic save and excludes Blaster; HP application stays manual. Native undo has the documented ordinary-HP limitation, requiring manual adjustment for temporary-HP-only undo. Uncertain chat publication is not retried automatically. Sources: [Blastback](../../src/scripts/features/blaster-blastback.ts), [visuals](../../src/scripts/features/blaster-blastback-visuals.ts), [operating instructions](../../README.md#blastback).

- **FR-012 — Resolution tools.** GM tools apply the selected existing blast rider using the adjudicated final outcome and expose an explicit counterblast card after a finished Blaster-winning Beam Clash. They do not intercept other modules' attacks or automatically determine incoming damage. Preserve existing slowing/hypnotic outcome grades, incapacitation handling and manual control behavior without transferring actor ownership. A won Blaster clash negates the incoming effect against Blaster only and permits the shipped counterblast without another attack/save roll or action debit; a loss uses the shipped critical incoming grade. Native spells retain their normal PF2e casting controls and uses independently of these macros. Sources: [resolution helper](../../src/scripts/features/blaster-resolution.ts), [Blaster actor](../../src/packs/astillon-blaster-actors.db/Blaster.json), [support effects](../../src/packs/astillon-blaster-items.db).

- **FR-013 — Managed Flash Mob.** The active GM chooses Hype Fan, Ego Guard, Paparazzo or Surprise Me and places one add through Summons Assistant. Creation uses the existing helper identity, summoner linkage, expiration and variant data; cancellation/failure must not knowingly duplicate a summon. Current mechanics have no live-thrall cap or combat/turn/frequency invocation locks. A placed add starts its signature support mode, has the shipped HP/lifetime, and gains no independent turn. Command changes one eligible owned add's mode for its remaining lifetime. Recovery repairs recognized managed adds and cleanup handles their death, expiry or deletion without adopting unrelated tokens. Sources: [summons](../../src/scripts/features/blaster-summons.ts), [thrall lifecycle](../../src/scripts/features/blaster-thralls.ts), [actor variants](../../src/packs/astillon-blaster-actors.db).

- **FR-014 — Support and manual GM placements.** Managed adds provide owner-linked native aura support. Inspire/Guard count qualifying emitters cumulatively; Bolster tracks one cumulative pool with per-source spending, spent FIFO, and leaving/re-entry restores only unspent contributions. Death, removal and mode changes update support. Separately, the GM may drag support auras/effects onto any actor: manual Inspire/Guard copies activate and stack, manual Bolster auras join the pool, and directly dropped Bolster effects use native PF2e temporary HP. Manual aura targeting currently defaults to bearer/allies; this is not a claim that every arbitrary edited targeting configuration is implemented. Manual placements remain distinct from summon cleanup. Sources: [aura totals](../../src/scripts/features/blaster-aura-totals.ts), [pool accounting](../../src/scripts/features/blaster-mob-pool.ts), [thrall handling](../../src/scripts/features/blaster-thralls.ts).

### Beam Clash and Dark Triad

- **FR-015 — Beam Clash.** A GM starts/stops a contest between one selected and one distinct targeted actor-backed token; neither Blaster identity, character type, combat, nor an online/assigned player owner is required. GMs may roll either side; other users may roll only owned sides. The active GM authenticates/serializes requests, revalidates authority after asynchronous work, and rejects duplicate/stale rolls. Each completed pair of unmodified d20s shifts the shared score toward the higher roller, with ties unchanged, until a winner. Persistent beams, token roll controls/indicators and the midpoint label display progress without start/roll/stop/victory chat chatter or an on-map counterblast button. Recovery and teardown protect against obsolete visual work and missing participants; unchanged effects are not needlessly restarted. Sources: [Beam Clash factory](../../src/scripts/features/beam-clash.ts), [contest model](../../src/scripts/features/beam-clash-model.ts).

- **FR-016 — Dark Triad cutscene.** GM Start creates a shared world-setting run after visual dependency checks. Clients render a persistent screen-space blackout, lightning and rotating image above the UI without creating map tiles or moving the camera. Thunder loops locally through each viewer's Environment channel with native low-pass filtering, volume/mute support and fades. GM End/Escape ends it globally; player Escape dismisses that run locally. Restart, dismissal and delayed audio/asset work have cancellation guards so an ended run cannot revive itself. Source: [Dark Triad factory](../../src/scripts/features/dark-triad-cutscene.ts).

### Updates and retained world data

- **FR-017 — Package versus world copies.** Updating the module refreshes its runtime/assets/compendia; reload connected clients. It does not generally replace actors, items or macro source already copied into a world. Runtime-delegating wrappers, including Sarsaparilla and Dark Triad, use updated module code after reload; self-contained imported race macros must be reimported for code changes. New controls require importing their macro entries. Stop/restart existing Beam Clashes after animation changes. Sources: [README](../../README.md), [player macro](../../src/packs/astillon-player-macros.db/Sarsaparilla%20Surprise.json), [race macros](../../src/packs/astillon-race-macros.db), [encounter macros](../../src/packs/astillon-blaster-macros.db).

- **FR-018 — Blaster update boundaries.** Refresh Sheet Descriptions replaces matching shipped description text only; it does not add missing abilities/spells or overwrite HP, statistics, artwork, effects, notes or other items. Upgrade Existing Sheet updates/adds recognized homebrew action/melee content and the designated chassis/turn sound, preserving wounds by default with an explicit full-heal option. It retains art, custom items, actor/ability GM notes, native spell uses and other Your Turn settings. It is not a complete replacement/import of native spellcasting; use a fresh import or deliberate transfer for missing spells. Existing marked summon repair and Your Turn setting migration are targeted recovery, not a general world migration. Sources: [description refresh](../../src/scripts/features/blaster.ts), [sheet upgrade](../../src/scripts/features/blaster-resolution.ts), [summons](../../src/scripts/features/blaster-summons.ts), [Your Turn](../../src/scripts/features/your-turn.ts).

## Governing obligations

The following are **DESIRED OBLIGATIONS**, including preservation of existing mechanisms. Source observations identify starting points, not a completed performance or compatibility certification.

**SHIPPED performance starting point, NOT LIVE VERIFIED:** 0.10.21 already batches/scopes aura reconciliation and shares provider snapshots, defers and bounds portrait preparation, filters unrelated updates, and targets Blastback movement updates to the affected charge. These source mechanisms are evidenced in [aura totals](../../src/scripts/features/blaster-aura-totals.ts), [summons hooks](../../src/scripts/features/blaster-summons.ts), [Your Turn](../../src/scripts/features/your-turn.ts), and [Blastback](../../src/scripts/features/blaster-blastback.ts). The obligations below preserve and extend that discipline without claiming measured speedups.

- **OB-001 — Preserve table authority and balance.** Preserve the user's current ability values, grades, stacking, costs and chosen exceptions. Do not introduce mechanics, action spending, combat/turn/frequency locks or a summon cap under the guise of adoption, validation or optimization. Authority, source identity, stale-operation, concurrency and rollback checks remain distinct from gameplay permission gates. The GM decides recipients of manually dragged effects/auras; do not veto, disable or remove these for lacking Blaster identity, summon linkage/markers, combat participation or because another effect exists. Managed cleanup must not consume manual placements.
- **OB-002 — Native contracts first.** Before future runtime or content work, consult the public Foundry documentation for the actual target build and the relevant current PF2e source/API. Preserve native documents, permission checks, conditions, aura geometry, roll controls and clickable rules syntax where applicable; do not invent APIs or replace native mechanics with unsupported substitutes. This documentation-only adoption does not renew the manifest's compatibility claim or require an upstream code update.
- **OB-003 — Performance hot paths.** Keep startup, token create/update/move, actor/effect changes, combat hooks, scene switches and reconnect handling proportional to affected work. Filter unrelated changes early; batch/coalesce relevant events; scope aura reconciliation to affected scenes/actors; reuse a provider snapshot within a pass, rebuilding only when required by state changes. Avoid routine world-wide actor/synthetic-token scans, repeated full compendium loads, redundant writes and unbounded background/image work. Preserve disabled-feature short circuits, deferred portrait preparation, current combat/scene priority, yielding image work, bounded prepared-art cache, and affected-charge-only Blastback movement updates. Current starting points: [aura totals](../../src/scripts/features/blaster-aura-totals.ts), [summons hooks](../../src/scripts/features/blaster-summons.ts), [Your Turn preparation](../../src/scripts/features/your-turn.ts), [Blastback](../../src/scripts/features/blaster-blastback.ts). These mechanisms do not establish measured latency, memory usage or frame rate.
- **OB-004 — Lifecycle and shared work.** Keep listeners, timers, requests, overlays, sounds, textures and pending asynchronous jobs owned by their feature/run. Cancel or ignore stale work; dispose local resources on the appropriate end/teardown path; retain intentionally shared cached assets safely. Maintain authoritative mutations and idempotent publication where implemented, and avoid automatic retries of uncertain external publication. GM handover/reconnect must not duplicate encounter actions. Do not claim every existing ledger/cache is bounded merely because some are.
- **OB-005 — Functional validation only in real Foundry.** Do not create, extend, update, run or delegate unit tests, mock/JSDOM suites, synthetic browser fixtures or content/statblock assertion suites unless the user explicitly asks. Only separately authorized functional checks in an actual Foundry instance establish behavior. When not performed, report **NOT LIVE VERIFIED**; source reading, simulated previews, historical suite results and compilation are not substitutes. Keep investigation and checks brief and relevant to the authorized change.
- **OB-006 — Packaging and CI.** Build/compile only to package an authorized release; packaging is not functional testing. Both [release CI](../../.github/workflows/main.yml) and [Build CI](../../.github/workflows/build.yml) package without automated suites as of 0.10.22. The stale Build workflow's `npm test` invocation was corrected during adoption. Historical README test claims are not current requirements. Deliver requested changes through scoped version/merge/push/publication without claiming live acceptance.

## Key persistent entities

- **Package content and world copies:** compendium documents retain collection/document identity; imported actors, items and self-contained macro commands are independently editable world data.

- **Contest state:** scene-linked participants, score, round, accepted rolls and request bookkeeping, with authoritative GM mutation and client-local rendering.

- **SC-004 — Performance accountability:** Later relevant work can be assessed against OB-003/004 and MA-011 without claiming unmeasured improvements or substituting synthetic benchmarks for actual Foundry behavior.
- **SC-005 — Bounded delivery:** Keep context and affected contracts current without duplicating history or expanding into unrelated implementation. Completing documentation does not imply runtime acceptance; the [adoption change spec](../002-spec-kit-adoption/spec.md) records scope and delivery separately.
